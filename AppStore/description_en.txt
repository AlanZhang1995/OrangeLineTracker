VTA Transit Tracker - Real-time Light Rail Arrivals for Apple Watch

Never miss your train again! VTA Transit Tracker brings real-time arrival information for Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority (VTA) Light Rail directly to your wrist.

FEATURES

• Real-Time Arrivals
Get accurate arrival predictions for all VTA Light Rail stations. See exactly how many minutes until your next train arrives.

• All Three Lines Supported
- Orange Line: Mountain View ↔ Alum Rock
- Blue Line: Baypointe ↔ Santa Teresa
- Green Line: Old Ironsides ↔ Winchester

• Smart Time Rules
Automation designed for commuters. Set up multiple time-based rules to automatically switch stations and directions:
- 7:30 AM: Auto-show your work-bound station and direction
- 5:30 PM: Auto-switch to your homebound station and direction
- Enable/disable rules anytime
No more manual switching - just raise your wrist to see what you need.

• Watch Face Widget
Add a complication to your watch face for instant access to arrival times. Supports Circular, Rectangular, Inline, and Corner styles.
Rectangular shows station name and direction; Circular shows countdown only. Recommend using Rectangular first to verify time rules work correctly, then switch to Circular.

• Bilingual Support
Full English and Chinese language support. Switch languages anytime in settings.

• Smart Refresh
Intelligent refresh intervals based on train proximity - more frequent updates when your train is close.
(Note: watchOS limits background refresh to ~15 min intervals, so widget data is approximate. If you need precise timing, open the app to confirm.)

• Custom API Key
Use your own free 511.org API key to avoid rate limiting.

PERFECT FOR

• Daily commuters on VTA Light Rail
• Visitors exploring Silicon Valley
• Anyone who wants quick access to train times

DATA SOURCE

Real-time data provided by 511.org SIRI API, the official source for Bay Area transit information.

PRIVACY

This app does not collect any personal data. All preferences are stored locally on your device.
